Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Gesture Arena uses your device camera for hand gesture recognition. Video is processed locally in your browser via MediaPipe and is not uploaded to our servers unless you enable multiplayer video chat (peer-to-peer between players).

Data we collect

  • Account email and username if you sign up (via Supabase)
  • Game statistics: wins, losses, streaks, match history
  • Avatar and nickname preferences stored on your device

Data we do not sell

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact the app administrator.
